Financial Literacy & Investor Rights

Price-to-Earnings (P/E) Multiple

Measures how much investors pay per rupee of net company profit. High P/E signifies high growth expectations. Always evaluate in the context of industry median and earnings growth (PEG).

Return on Capital Employed (ROCE)

Assesses how efficiently total capital (equity + long term debt) generates operating profit (EBIT). Consistent ROCE > 18% indicates high capital efficiency and pricing power.
